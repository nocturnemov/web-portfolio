HR Space

Recruitment & Executive HR Consultancy Platform · Dar es Salaam, Tanzania

Community & Media Platform · Sermons, Live Stream & Events

Engineered a full-featured web portal for a large regional congregation. Replaced fragmented social posts with a centralized, mobile-responsive hub for live sermon broadcasts, event registrations, ministry information, and online giving.

  • Interactive community prayer request intake and online giving modules.
