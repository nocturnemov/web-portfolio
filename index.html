About Me

LOC: Dar es Salaam / Global
STATUS: Hobbyist Coder & Tinkerer

Coding is my creative playground. I don't treat web development as a stiff corporate routine — for me, building websites and web apps is an addictive hobby and a genuine creative outlet.

It all started as late-night curiosity: inspecting random websites, messing around with CSS until layouts broke, and writing small scripts just to see what would happen. That itch never went away. Today, whenever I get some free downtime, my favorite way to unwind is opening up a code editor, firing up Next.js and TypeScript, and hacking away on something new.

Even though I build purely for the fun of it, I'm pretty obsessive about the details. I love clean code that just works, pages that load in the blink of an eye, and interfaces that feel snappy and delightful to click through. Whether I'm building a live platform for a church, helping friends get their projects online, or just experimenting with interactive UI ideas, I build because there’s nothing quite like the rush of making something out of nothing and putting it on the internet.

HR Space

Recruitment & Consultancy Web Platform · Dar es Salaam, Tanzania

A fun, full-scale web project I took on for a recruitment team in Dar es Salaam. Instead of a bland static brochure, I wanted to build them an engaging, interactive space with clean service breakdowns, an animated candidate visualizer, and simple ways for clients to reach out.

Put together with Next.js App Router and Turbopack because I love instant page transitions and quick local builds. I paired Space Grotesk and Inter typography with responsive CSS grids, adding smooth SVG animations that give the blueprint theme some personality.

  • Interactive Candidate Matching visualizer with animated blueprint elements.
  • Clear, modular walkthroughs of their 4 main services.
  • Direct, hassle-free contact pathways (direct booking, email, phone, location map).

Faith Victory Church

Community & Media Portal · Sermons, Live Stream & Events

Built this platform to help a regional church bring their community experience together online. Rather than juggling scattered social posts, members and visitors can easily tune into live broadcast streams, catch up on past sermons, submit prayer requests, or check what's happening this weekend.

Built on Next.js 15 with server-side rendering so stream statuses and schedule updates reflect instantly. I paid extra attention to media optimization, making sure video and audio streams start quickly and feel smooth even on spotty mobile connections.

  • Dedicated live broadcast page (`/watch-live`) with real-time stream status.
  • Searchable sermon archive and media library for on-demand playback.
  • Interactive community prayer request submissions and giving portal.
  • Simple admin dashboard (`/admin`) for staff to update schedules and media.
